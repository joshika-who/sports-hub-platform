# Return-to-Play (RTP) Progression & Readiness Criteria

## The Control-Chaos Continuum
Returning to competitive sport too early is the #1 risk factor for re-injury. Instead of relying solely on time elapsed, athletes should clear objective clinical and functional benchmarks before full tournament participation.

## 5 Objective Return-to-Play Criteria
1. **Zero Swelling & Full Range of Motion:** No joint effusion after training; symmetrical passive and active range of motion compared to the uninjured side.
2. **Limb Symmetry Index (LSI >= 90–95%):**
   - Isometric and eccentric strength of the injured limb must reach at least 90% (95% for pivoting sports like football/badminton) of the uninjured limb.
   - Hop Test Battery: Single hop for distance, triple hop, crossover hop, and 6-meter timed hop all achieving >=90% LSI.
3. **Pain-Free Sport-Specific Movement:** Zero pain (<1/10 VAS) during maximal deceleration, cutting, jumping, or overhead serving.
4. **Acute:Chronic Workload Ratio (ACWR):** Gradually rebuild training volume over 2–4 weeks so weekly load stays within the "sweet spot" of 0.8 to 1.3 relative to the 4-week rolling chronic average.
5. **Psychological Readiness:** High confidence in the injured joint/muscle without kinesiophobia (fear of movement or hesitation during contact).

## Staged Field/Court Progression
- **Stage 1:** Linear running & controlled footwork (predictable environment).
- **Stage 2:** Multi-directional agility & planned cutting drills.
- **Stage 3:** Reactive agility & unopposed team drills (chaotic cues).
- **Stage 4:** Restricted-minute match return before full tournament load.
